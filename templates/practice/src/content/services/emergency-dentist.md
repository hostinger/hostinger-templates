## Start with a phone call

Dental pain can make every decision feel urgent. A short call helps us understand the symptoms, timing, and any swelling or injury before you travel.

### Before your visit

- Note when symptoms began and what makes them better or worse
- Bring a list of current medications and allergies
- Avoid placing aspirin directly on gums or teeth
- Arrange a ride if the team tells you sedation may be discussed

This page is general information, not diagnosis. For difficulty breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, or severe facial trauma, call emergency services.
