## Repairs with context

Our goal is to preserve healthy tooth structure and recommend only what the evidence supports. You will see the relevant X-rays or photos and receive an explanation of each reasonable option.

### Your comfort plan

- Choose whether you want each step explained or prefer fewer details
- Use an agreed hand signal to pause at any time
- Confirm numbness before treatment begins
- Leave with written aftercare and a number to call

Online information cannot determine which treatment is right for you. An in-person exam is required before any diagnosis or estimate.
