---
name: Formstacker
category: Forms
icon: formstacker
accent: tangerine
summary: Turn form responses into assigned, validated workflows the moment someone submits.
trigger: Form response submitted
action: Start Relay workflow
useCases:
  - Convert partner applications into review tasks.
  - Route intake requests by answer, territory, or urgency.
  - Send a confirmation after a complete submission.
setup:
  - Select the Formstacker form you want to watch.
  - Match response fields to Relay workflow inputs.
  - Submit a sample response and confirm its route.
faqs:
  - question: What happens to incomplete responses?
    answer: Only submitted responses trigger Relay; draft responses remain in Formstacker.
  - question: Can one form start different workflows?
    answer: Yes. Branch on any response field to route submissions to distinct workflows.
---
Formstacker bridges structured intake and accountable follow-through. Every submission arrives with its original answers, route, and next owner.
