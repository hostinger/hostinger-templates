Afterimage House occupies a former print workshop where artists are invited to make work in public. The identity needed to hold both the building’s industrial memory and a programme that changes shape every six weeks.

## A system that leaves a trace

The visual language begins with registration marks, overprinted colour and captions that refuse the centre. Electric blue identifies the institution; vermilion moves through each season as an active curatorial voice. A flexible wordmark sits quietly while project titles grow to architectural scale.

Across printed matter, wayfinding and screen, every composition holds one deliberate misalignment. The result feels collected rather than polished: an identity that remembers the hand, the press and the room.
